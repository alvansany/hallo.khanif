## 3. Case Study Breakdown (Design Thinking Framework)

### A. Empathize (User Understanding)
- **Problem:** Fragmented healthcare services in Indonesia.
- **User Needs:**
    - **Patients:** Fast access to teleconsultation (Medevo) and medicine delivery (Medpharm).
    - **Doctors:** Efficient tools for digital prescriptions and patient history (EHR).
    - **Merchants (Pharmacy):** Real-time inventory and order management.

### B. Define (Strategy & Logic)
- **Business Process Alignment:** Translating complex BUMN operational requirements into streamlined digital flows.
- **Information Architecture:** Managing massive data flows between different stakeholders (Patients <-> Doctors <-> Pharmacies).

### C. Ideate (Architecture & Flow)
- **Wireframing:** Focus on minimizing friction in the checkout and booking flows.
- **Omnichannel Approach:** Ensuring a consistent experience across Native Apps and PWA for accessibility.

### D. Prototype (Visual & Systematic Execution)
- **Design System:** Created a centralized library of reusable components to ensure consistency across 5+ platforms.
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, Creative, and Professional. High emphasis on trust and clarity.
- **Adaptability:** Full support for Light Mode and Dark Mode.
- **Tech-Stack Awareness:** Design implementation with consideration for modern UI frameworks (e.g., Chakra UI).

### E. Test & Impact (Result)
- **Product Launch:** Successfully launched on App Store & Play Store.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Streamlined the digital prescription flow and pharmacy delivery (aiming for <60 mins delivery).
- **Scalability:** Provided a robust design foundation for future feature expansions.
